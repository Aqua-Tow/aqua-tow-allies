What is an Ally?

Aqua-Tow Allies is a directory, not a towing service. It's a way for people who already own an Aqua-Tow kit to let nearby boaters know they're around and willing to help — nothing more. Aqua-Tow doesn't dispatch anyone, doesn't perform any tow, and isn't a party to what happens between two boaters who connect through this page.

What Aqua-Tow verifies

Only that the person listed as an Ally purchased a genuine Aqua-Tow product — the Tow System, Jump Kit, Pump Kit, or the Full Recovery Kit bundle. That's the entire verification. Aqua-Tow does not background-check, license, certify, train, insure, supervise, or dispatch Allies, and does not verify boating experience, vessel condition, or towing capability.

An Ally is not part of Aqua-Tow

Being listed as an Ally does not make someone an employee, contractor, partner, representative, or agent of Aqua-Tow. Allies act entirely on their own behalf, using their own equipment and their own judgment.

Liability is between the two boaters

Any towing, assistance, or service that results from a connection made on this page is a private arrangement solely between the boater requesting help and the Ally providing it. Aqua-Tow is not a party to that arrangement and assumes no responsibility or liability for it — including, without limitation, personal injury, vessel or property damage, equipment failure, or disputes over cost, timing, or quality of service. Each boater is responsible for confirming their own insurance coverage and for deciding whether to give or accept help.

Towing carries real risk

Towing another vessel on the water can be dangerous if done incorrectly. Both the towing boater and the boater being towed are responsible for using proper equipment, following safe towing practices, and using good judgment about conditions, speed, and load before attempting a tow.

This page is general information provided for boaters and Allies, not legal advice, and doesn't replace formal Terms of Service or a liability waiver — Aqua-Tow recommends having this reviewed by an attorney before it's treated as the final legal language for the network.

How the network works

1
You buy a kit.

The Tow System, Jump Kit, Pump Kit, or all three as the Full Recovery Kit — every purchase comes with an order number.

2
You opt in.

Submit your order number, phone, and a general location — not your exact address, just an area boaters would recognize (a lake, marina, or town).

3
We verify.

Aqua-Tow checks the order number against real purchase records before your pin goes live — this keeps the network limited to actual kit owners. It's a purchase check, not a background check.

4
You're listed.

Your pin shows your tow capacity, your preferred way to be reached, and — if you choose to add them — a short bio and profile picture. Nothing tracks your live position — you set the pin, and can move or remove it anytime.

5
You control when you're shown.

Set your available hours and you'll only appear in nearby searches during that window — no calls at 2am unless you want them.

6
Someone nearby needs help.

They open this page, share their location once, and see the closest Allies with distance and a one-tap call or text — and connect directly with them.

Being an Ally is optional and separate from the kit itself — the product works on its own. This is a bonus for owners willing to lend a hand.
